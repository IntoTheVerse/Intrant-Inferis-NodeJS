## Features: 
- 📱 Third Person Dungeon Battler (on-chain PvE) <br />
- 🪖 Programmable warriors - dynamic cNFTS whose attributes, wearables, weapons, and level progression are programmed using Bubblegum on Solana - kudos to Metaplex and Underdog protocol <br />
- 🏅 On-chain leaderboard to reflect player performances and reputation aiding in incentive distribution - shoutout to MagicBlock Labs SOAR framework <br />
- ⚰️🧪 Reversible Permadeath - Warriors freeze for 2 hours after death, but can be revived on-chain using potions (cNFTs) <br />
- 💎 Artefacts as collectibles - Artifacts are collected during raids as cNFTs which are then used to craft potions, powerups and pocket pals using various combinations inside the Codex (Brewery + Inventory) <br />
- 🛍️📦 Markeplace & Inventory - In game marketplace and inventory - trade and manage earned assets and equip on demand <br />
- 🏁 Tokenised Checkpoints - Level/Map progression are stored and updated as cNFTs on Solana <br />
- 🧙‍♀️ Crafting and Brewing - Different combinations of collected cNFT artifacts, products, potions and powerups are used as temporary and permanent boosts respectively <br />
- 📁 Wallets and account abstraction - Sign in with Phantom or using your google account on Web3 Auth and you do not have to worry about gas fees, in-game transaction signatures and seed-phrases or keys <br />
