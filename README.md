## Features: 
- 📱 Third Person Dungeon Battler built for mobile (on-chain PvE rogue-like mechanics)
- 💰 In game chests to collect loot in BONK tokens - these chests spawn randomly and may reward you or steak your loot by spawning goblin thieves
- 🎮 On-chain raid tournaments with leaderboard & automated reward distribution in $BONK tokens - that utlises game reputation (no of kills, time taken for completion, effective attacks, chests collected) and translates it to an on-chain reputation using Magicblock SOAR (Solana On-chain Reputation and Rewards) framework to manage automated reward distribution in $BONK tokens to the leaders, upon expiry of weekly tournaments 
- 🪖 Programmable warriors - dynamic cNFTS whose attributes, wearables, weapons, and level progression are programmed using Bubblegum on Solana - kudos to Metaplex and Underdog protocol
- ⚰️🧪 Reversible Permadeath - Warriors freeze for 2 hours after death, but can be revived on-chain using potions (cNFTs)
- 💎 Artefacts as collectibles - Artifacts are collected during raids as cNFTs which are then used to craft potions, powerups and pocket pals using various combinations inside the Codex (Brewery + Inventory)
- 🏁 Tokenised Checkpoints - Level/Map progression are stored and updated as cNFTs on Solana - so that you can load from a preious game state by checking for the cNFT in your wallet
- 🧙‍♀️ Crafting and Brewing - Different combinations of collected cNFT artifacts, products, potions and powerups are used as temporary and permanent boosts respectively
- 📁 Wallets and account abstraction - Sign in with Phantom or using your google account on Web3 Auth and you do not have to worry about gas fees, in-game transaction signatures and seed-phrases or keys
- ⛓️ Game runs at web2 speeds powered by web3 infrastructure like MagicBlock Session Keys - that abstract the need for wallet popups and signatures
